Credit and answers the same day, for customers who already bank with you.

SameDay Plus is a proposed membership inside a banking app. It looks at six months of your own account activity, offers a limit in minutes, and puts a person behind every answer from the assistant.

This is a classroom concept. No invitations are issued and no information is collected on this page.

Decision in under 5 minutes

Your limit comes from the money that moves through your account, so a payslip from one employer is not the only proof.

One price, shown first

You see the full cost of a loan before you accept it. No fee appears later.

A person if the assistant cannot help

If the assistant cannot resolve your case, a team member calls back within 2 hours.

Who it is for

Adults in Nigeria with steady income paid into their account for at least six months, who open the app each week and need a small short-term loan, roughly ₦50,000 to ₦500,000.

It is not designed for cash-only customers, businesses, or people without verifiable income.

What you get as a member

Two things change: how fast you get an answer, and how credit is decided.

Assistant first, person guaranteed

The assistant answers routine questions at any hour using approved bank wording. When it cannot resolve a case, it hands over with your details so you do not repeat yourself.

Credit from your cash flow

With your consent, the bank reviews six months of inflows to set a limit. Repay automatically or manually, with reminders before each due date.

A person decides what matters most

  • Appeals after a declined application, reviewed within 1 working day on request.
  • Complaints about lost money or suspected fraud.
  • Repayment difficulty and hardship plans.

Two prices, both visible before you start

Standard

₦0

Your normal account and the assistant. Credit is not included.

Plus

₦1,000 a month

Priority callbacks, appeals review, and access to cash-flow credit.

Credit price

Interest is 2.95% a month on the amount you borrow. The total is shown to you before you accept.

How your data and your decisions are handled

Consent first

The cash-flow review happens only after you agree, and follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Limits on automation

The assistant does not decide appeals, complaints or collections. People do.

Fair outcomes

Approval rates are checked each quarter by income type, and the model is validated every year.

What this page is

SameDay Plus is a proposal prepared for a university business course. Prices, service times and eligibility are modelled targets that would be tested with customer interviews and a pilot before any launch. This page collects no data.
